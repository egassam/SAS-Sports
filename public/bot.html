About the SAS Sports fetcher

SAS Sports is a fan app that shows schedules and results for college teams. It reads the public schedule, roster and recap pages that schools publish on their official athletics sites, and links back to them.

How it identifies itself

Every request carries the user agent Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; SAS-Sports/<version>; +https://sas-sports.lovetogivepain.workers.dev/bot). It never presents itself as a browser.

How often it visits

  • Each page is downloaded once and reused: schedule pages for 2 minutes, news listings and rosters for 10 minutes, articles for 30 minutes, documents for 6 hours.
  • Expired copies are checked with If-None-Match / If-Modified-Since, so an unchanged page costs a 304.
  • A 429 or 503 stops requests to that page until its Retry-After ends, with at least a minute's pause. Any other refusal pauses them for 15 seconds.
  • Visitors to SAS Sports are served from these saved copies. They never cause extra requests to your site.

robots.txt

The fetcher obeys robots.txt. Rules for User-agent: SAS-Sports take priority over the rules for *. To keep it off some or all of your site, add a group for SAS-Sports; it is re-read daily.
