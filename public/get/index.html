Install it

SAS Sports installs from your web browser and sits on your home screen like any other app. It updates itself.

Before you start

  • Use Safari, the iPhone's own browser (the blue compass). It's the surest way. If someone texted you this link, tapping it in Messages opens Safari already.
  • Opened the link inside Instagram, Facebook, Snapchat, TikTok or X? Those apps use their own browser, which can't install apps. Tap the ··· menu (top or bottom right) and choose Open in Safari or Open in external browser. Or copy this link and paste it into Safari: sas-sports.lovetogivepain.workers.dev/get/

Install on iPhone

  1. In Safari, find the Share button (a square with an arrow pointing up).
    • Bottom toolbar (most iPhones): it's in the middle of the bar at the bottom of the screen. If the bar is hidden, tap the bottom of the screen or scroll up a little to bring it back.
    • iOS 26, or when the address bar is at the bottom with no toolbar: tap next to the address, then tap Share.
  2. A sheet slides up. Scroll down past the row of contacts and apps to the list of actions, and tap Add to Home Screen.
    • Don't see it? Scroll all the way down, tap Edit Actions…, and add Add to Home Screen.
  3. Leave the name as SAS Sports. If you see Open as Web App, leave it switched on. Tap Add at the top right.
  4. The SAS icon is now on your home screen (swipe over if your first page is full). Always open SAS Sports from that icon, not from Safari: that's what makes it the app, full screen and with alerts.

iPad

The same steps, but the Share button is at the top right, next to the address bar.

Turn on game alerts (optional)

  1. Open SAS Sports from the home screen icon and star your teams with ☆ Favorite.
  2. Tap Alerts at the top, then Allow when your iPhone asks.
  3. You'll get one alert when a favorite's game starts and one with the final score.

Alerts need iOS 16.4 or newer (Settings → General → About → iOS Version). Tapped Don't Allow by mistake? Go to Settings → Notifications → SAS Sports and turn on Allow Notifications.

Using Chrome on iPhone?

It works too on iOS 16.4 or newer: tap in the address bar, then Add to Home Screen. If you don't see it, use Safari.

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install the app at the top of this page, or open Chrome's ⋮ menu and tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Tap Install. SAS Sports shows up with your other apps.
  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click Install the app at the top of this page, or the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  3. Click Install. It opens in its own window. On a Mac with Safari, use File → Add to Dock.

Don't want to install? Everything works at the website too. Bookmark it.

How it works

Pick a team and it's all there: what's next, what happened, and what's live.

🏟Four levels

Tap Middle School, High School, College or Pro at the top. Each one opens that level's own page with this week's games, then pick a conference or city and a team.

⌂Home

Live now across every level, your teams' next game, and the week ahead (Monday to Sunday) with times, TV, lines and records where they're published.

📋Team pages

Choose a sport, then Live, Results, Upcoming, News and Roster. Tap any game to open it: box score, recap, where it's played and more.

✓Easy to read

Finals say who won (W 28–21), every game is marked HOME or AWAY, and live games show LIVE with the score as it happens.

🔗Official sources

Schedules and results come from the schools' and leagues' own sites and scoring services, checked and refreshed all day. Tap ↻ to check again right now.

🏃Every sport

Football to cross country, golf, tennis, wrestling, swimming and track: meets show places and times, not just scores.

Make it yours

Five minutes of setup and the app opens on exactly what you care about. Your choices stay on your phone.

1

Star your teams

Open a team and tap ☆ Favorite. Star as many as you like, at any level. Your teams lead Home with a gold ★ My Teams banner.

2

Put them in order

Open the My Teams list to jump straight to a team, move teams ↑ up or ↓ down, or remove one with ✕.

3

Choose what Home shows

Tap ⚙ Customize at the top of Home and pick the sports you want at each level, say Pro Football and College Basketball. Everyone else's games follow your picks; your starred teams always show. Each level page has its own Customize. Show everything resets it.

4

Turn on alerts

Tap Alerts to get a notification when a favorite's game starts and another with the final score. Works for pro, college, high school and middle school teams. On iPhone, install the app first.

5

Add games to your calendar

Tap Calendar to subscribe to your teams' schedules in Apple, Google or Outlook. Times update on their own and finals add their scores.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. It's free, with no account and no sign-in.

Why isn't it in the App Store or Google Play?

It installs straight from your browser instead, so it's the same app on every phone and computer, and it updates the moment something new ships. No downloads to manage.

Will it use up my phone's storage?

Barely. It keeps a small copy of your teams' last results so pages open fast, and cleans that up on its own.

My school or team isn't in it. Can you add it?

Very likely. Use the form below, pick Missing team, and tell us the school, the city and state, and the sports.

A score or time looks wrong.

Tap ↻ on the team page to check the official source again. If it's still wrong, send it below with the team, the sport and the game date.

How do I remove it?

Delete it like any other app: press and hold the icon, then Remove (iPhone) or Uninstall (Android). On a computer, open the app's menu and choose Uninstall.
