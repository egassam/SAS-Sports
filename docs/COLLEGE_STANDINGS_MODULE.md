# College conference standings

User, November 11, 2026: "Start the college conference standings" (the next step after the auto racing standings, PR #580, and the pro team league standings, PR #584).

## What a page shows

A college school's **Football**, **Basketball**, **Hockey** (from College Hockey News, below) and **Volleyball** (from its conference's own site, below) sections show a Standings block (the same block as the pro pages): the school's conference by ESPN's seed, the top 5 with Show all, the school's own row marked and kept visible below the top 5.

- Columns: **Conf** (conference record), **Ovr** (overall record; "Overall" ran into "Strk" at 390 px), **Strk** (streak). The detail line under the name is the home and away record.
- Football conferences with divisions (Sun Belt, SWAC) show the school's division first, then the whole conference.
- Basketball shows two tabs, "Men's <conference>" and "Women's <conference>" (one when the school has no women's team: The Citadel, VMI).
- Source line: "ESPN standings (2026 season)" / "(2026-27 season)", linked to ESPN's conference page.

## Source and reads

- `https://site.api.espn.com/apis/v2/sports/<board>/standings?group=<conference id>` for `football/college-football`, `basketball/mens-college-basketball`, `basketball/womens-college-basketball`. One read per conference (about 300 KB), kept 30 minutes (`SOURCE_TTL.article`) and shared by every school in it, sent with the scoreboard identity (`readJson`).
- The whole-division payloads (FBS 2.6 MB, FCS 2.4 MB, basketball about 6 MB, 34 ms to parse in Node) are never read by the Worker: parsing them in a feed build risks Cloudflare 1102 (CPU).
- Which conference each school is in: `src/college-standings.json`, `{board: {school id: [ESPN group, ESPN team id]}}`, generated by `node scripts/make-college-standings.mjs` from the whole-division reads (FBS default, FCS `group=81`). It matches teams with the app's scoreboard matcher (`scoreboardTeamMatchesSchool`), refuses one ESPN team for two schools, and lists schools it cannot map. November 11, 2026: football 232 schools (23 conferences), men's basketball 315, women's 313 (32 conferences each).
- The school's own row is found by ESPN team id; a school missing from its conference's read (realignment) shows no table. **Rerun the generator each July (realignment) and after adding college schools**; `tests/college-standings.mjs` fails for a college school with football or basketball and no entry.

## Not mapped (by design)

- Football independents (Notre Dame, UConn, Merrimack, Chicago State): ESPN's "FBS/FCS Independents" is not a conference, so no table.
- Women's basketball: The Citadel and VMI have no women's team.

## Scoreboard names (fixed November 12, 2026)

The scoreboard matcher (`scoreboardTeamMatchesSchool`) missed three schools by name, found by this module on November 11: UC Davis (its alias "UC-Davis" read as a nickname and removed its own name "UC Davis"), Southeastern Louisiana (ESPN "SE Louisiana") and USC Upstate (ESPN "South Carolina Upstate"). Fixed in 4.302.1: a school's own name and short name are never treated as a nickname, and the catalog carries ESPN's spellings as aliases. The generator no longer needs ESPN team-id overrides; rerun on November 12, it gave the same map as before (0 differences on all three boards). On the test payloads the only new matches are those three schools' own teams (`tests/scoreboard-match.mjs`). Their live scores now join their official games on ESPN's boards as well.

## Hockey (November 12, 2026)

User: "Add the hockey conference standings". ESPN's hockey standings cannot rank a conference: men's (`hockey/mens-college-hockey/standings`) groups teams by conference but each entry carries only the overall W-L-T (no `vsconf`, `points` 0.0, `playoffseed` 0, `streak` "-"; checked Nov 12 on the whole payload and `?group=54` CCHA, `?group=62` Big Ten), and women's has no conferences (one flat list of 45 teams). Hockey tables come from **College Hockey News** instead: one page per gender holds every Division I conference (`https://www.collegehockeynews.com/reports/standings.php`, about 120 KB; `/women/standings.php`, about 70 KB; robots.txt allows both), read with `readText` through the source cache (30 minutes, shared by every school, last good copy on a failed read).

- Columns: GP, Pts (conference), Ovr (overall W-L-T); the detail line is the conference regulation and overtime record ("Reg 4-0 · OT 0-0"). CHN's order (its tie-breaks); a tied team shares the rank above (CHN leaves the cell blank). Men's and Women's tabs for a school with both teams, the men's first.
- Map: `src/college-standings.json` boards `chn/mens-hockey` and `chn/womens-hockey`, `{school: [conference, CHN team id]}`, from the generator with the app's matcher on CHN's full names; four CHN spellings are named in the generator (`CHN_NAMES`: "Miami" is Miami (OH) and never Miami (FL), "Massachusetts", "Mass.-Lowell", women's "Long Island"). 33 men's and 26 women's teams mapped (Nov 12).
- Not mapped, by design: men's independents (LIU, Mercyhurst, Stonehill, Lindenwood; CHN's "Independent" is no conference), schools without a team of that gender, and Delaware men's (not on CHN's men's page in 2026-27). Jacksonville, NJIT and Tennessee State list Hockey in `sponsored-sports.json` but have no Division I team on either page.
- `src/college-standings.mjs`: `COLLEGE_HOCKEY_PAGES`, `parseChnStandings`, `collegeHockeyStandings`, `collegeHockeyReads`; `attachCollegeStandings` reads both kinds.

## Volleyball (November 12, 2026)

User: "Find another source for the other sports", then "Start with volleyball". Sources checked first: ESPN (`volleyball/womens-college-volleyball/standings`: one Division I table, no conferences), NCAA.com (`/standings/volleyball-women/d1`, every conference: the table region is empty, "Last Updated EDT" with no date; only football's pages hold data), Warren Nolan (baseball and softball only). The conferences' own sites publish the tables: **31 of the 32 conferences the app's schools play in run SIDEARM** (`https://<site>/standings.aspx?path=wvball`, the current season by default; robots.txt allows it). The SEC (secsports.com) is not SIDEARM and loads its standings by script: no table.

- Read: `conferenceStandingsUrl(conference, sport)` from `CONFERENCE_SITES` (catalog conference → host) and `CONFERENCE_STANDINGS_SPORTS` (`Volleyball: wvball`), per-conference codes in `CONFERENCE_PATHS` (Horizon League `vball`: its `wvball` answers SIDEARM's "Something went wrong"). One read per conference, 30 minutes, shared (`readText`, stale on error).
- Parse (`parseSidearmStandings`): the table's full column set (`hide-on-medium-down`). Columns are chosen by header and value, since each conference names them: the conference record is the first record-valued column that is not a percentage, games played, points, division, overall, home/away/neutral, last 10, streak or sets (seen: "Conf", "Conference", "Conf. W-L", "ACC", "Metro", "Big South", "BW", "SLC"); overall is "Overall"/"Ovr"/"All", else the next "W-L" (ASUN); the last "Streak"/"Strk"; Home and Away after overall. Names: the row's link text; clinched/eliminated marks stripped ("Cornell *", "Fairfield *#", "Manhattan E", "UMBC -xy"). Division groups (`<th scope="colgroup">`, Sun Belt) give the school's division tab first, then the whole conference by conference record.
- The school's row (`conferenceRowMatcher` in the Worker): the scoreboard matcher, or any catalog name or alias exactly (abbreviations too: "UNI", "EKU"), or the name without its state ("Miami (OH)" → MAC "Miami") or a trailing "U" ("Seattle U" → WCC "Seattle"). Safe only within one conference's page.
- `/live/status?school=&sport=` shows `standings_reads`: the page's HTTP answer, whether it holds a table, its title, row names and the school's row (and the start of the page when no row was read).

**Coverage (preview, Nov 12, every college volleyball school's feed):** 221 of 301 schools in 25 conferences, every school there on its own row, no row claimed twice. Not covered:

| Conference | Schools | Answer to the app |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ACC, Atlantic 10, Big 12, Conference USA, OVC | 57 | Imperva's "Too many redirects" (the block in `docs/BLOCKED_SCHOOLS.md`); not routed around |
| SEC | 16 | Not SIDEARM; standings load by script (no reader) |
| Southern Conference | 7 | `standings.aspx` answers HTTP 500 "Something went wrong" for `wvball`, `vball`, `wvb`, `volley` and with no path (also to WebFetch, Nov 12) |

Conference rows with no catalog volleyball school are mostly schools that block the app (Butler, Georgetown, Villanova, App State, Georgia State, Hawaiʻi, Nevada, Utah State, Texas State) or schools whose `sponsored-sports.json` entry has no Volleyball (Yale, Harvard, Iona, Saint Peter's, Canisius, Bradley, Drake, UIC, Wyoming, Little Rock).
