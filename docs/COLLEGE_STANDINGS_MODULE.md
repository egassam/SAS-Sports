# College conference standings

User, November 11, 2026: "Start the college conference standings" (the next step after the auto racing standings, PR #580, and the pro team league standings, PR #584).

## What a page shows

A college school's **Football** and **Basketball** sections show a Standings block (the same block as the pro pages): the school's conference by ESPN's seed, the top 5 with Show all, the school's own row marked and kept visible below the top 5.

- Columns: **Conf** (conference record), **Ovr** (overall record; "Overall" ran into "Strk" at 390 px), **Strk** (streak). The detail line under the name is the home and away record.
- Football conferences with divisions (Sun Belt, SWAC) show the school's division first, then the whole conference.
- Basketball shows two tabs, "Men's <conference>" and "Women's <conference>" (one when the school has no women's team: The Citadel, VMI).
- Source line: "ESPN standings (2026 season)" / "(2026-27 season)", linked to ESPN's conference page.

## Source and reads

- `https://site.api.espn.com/apis/v2/sports/<board>/standings?group=<conference id>` for `football/college-football`, `basketball/mens-college-basketball`, `basketball/womens-college-basketball`. One read per conference (about 300 KB), kept 30 minutes (`SOURCE_TTL.article`) and shared by every school in it, sent with the scoreboard identity (`readJson`).
- The whole-division payloads (FBS 2.6 MB, FCS 2.4 MB, basketball about 6 MB, 34 ms to parse in Node) are never read by the Worker: parsing them in a feed build risks Cloudflare 1102 (CPU).
- Which conference each school is in: `src/college-standings.json`, `{board: {school id: [ESPN group, ESPN team id]}}`, generated by `node scripts/make-college-standings.mjs` from the whole-division reads (FBS default, FCS `group=81`). It matches teams with the app's scoreboard matcher (`scoreboardTeamMatchesSchool`), refuses one ESPN team for two schools, and lists schools it cannot map. November 11, 2026: football 232 schools (23 conferences), men's basketball 315, women's 313 (32 conferences each).
- The school's own row is found by ESPN team id; a school missing from its conference's read (realignment) shows no table. **Rerun the generator each July (realignment) and after adding college schools**; `tests/college-standings.mjs` fails for a college school with football or basketball and no entry.

## Not mapped (by design)

- Football independents (Notre Dame, UConn, Merrimack, Chicago State): ESPN's "FBS/FCS Independents" is not a conference, so no table.
- Women's basketball: The Citadel and VMI have no women's team.

## Found, not fixed

- The scoreboard matcher misses three schools by name: UC Davis (its alias "UC-Davis" is read as a nickname, which removes its own name "UC Davis"), Southeastern Louisiana (ESPN "SE Louisiana") and USC Upstate (ESPN "South Carolina Upstate"). The generator maps them by ESPN team id (302, 2545, 2908). Their live scoreboard and poll matching likely have the same gap; not changed here (a shared matcher change needs `diff:feeds`).

## Next

- Other sports ESPN publishes: men's college hockey has conference tables (seeds not published; W-L-T records), women's hockey has a payload; volleyball and baseball have one Division I table only (no conferences); soccer and softball none. Their conference standings would need another source (the conferences' own sites).
