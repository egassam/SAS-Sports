# Geary County, Kansas: middle and high schools

User, November 3, 2026: "Let's add another county in Kansas", then (asked which) "Geary".

Riley and Pottawatomie counties were already in the app (`docs/RILEY_POTTAWATOMIE_KS_MODULE.md`). This session adds every middle and high school in Geary County that fields teams. Code: `src/schools/data/ks-geary.json` (Junction City HS, Junction City MS and Fort Riley MS as data since November 4, `docs/HIGH_SCHOOL_ONBOARDING.md`), `src/schools/geary-ks.mjs` (St. Xavier HS and JH; it reuses the Bound builders exported from `src/schools/riley-pottawatomie-ks.mjs`) and the shared readers in `src/high-school.mjs`; Worker path `fetchHighSchool` in `src/index.js`. The rules for middle and high schools are `AGENTS.md` item 5c.

## Rules

- High school: Bound's `Varsity` level. Sports: football, volleyball, boys' soccer (fall) and girls' soccer (spring), basketball, baseball, softball, cross country, golf (girls fall, boys spring), tennis (girls fall, boys spring; TennisReporting), wrestling, track and field, swimming and diving (boys winter, girls spring), bowling (girls, boys, Unified). `boundHighSchool` gained girls' soccer, swimming and boys'/girls' bowling, and a `paths` option for a MaxPreps path; the Riley and Pottawatomie schools read exactly as before (`tests/geary-ks-module.mjs` checks Wamego's bowling and St. Mary's Academy's soccer).
- Middle schools: the grades 7th and 8th and their A teams; Junction City and Fort Riley also write the A team "7th Grade - A - 1" and "7A" (`usd475Grade`); B teams are not listed. `boundMiddleSchool` gained Tennis (Centennial League middle school tennis; levels "Middle School", "MS", "MS A Team", grade). Golf is read as for Wamego MS.
- St. Xavier's basketball (`maxprepsSchedule`): the team's MaxPreps schedule is its schedule: finals with scores, coming games, and past games without a score as "No score posted". Volleyball reads as Junction City's: MaxPreps' finals, then Bound's varsity entries (October 19 vs Veritas Christian, no score posted). Its meets come from Bound plus the meets MileSplit Live has its runners at that Bound leaves out (October 3, 8 and 24); the same fills Junction City HS's 6A Sub-State (October 24) and Fort Riley MS's Clay Center meet (September 15). Since November 4 these come from the statewide MileSplit Live index (`docs/HIGH_SCHOOL_ONBOARDING.md`), no longer from a hand-kept `extraMeets` list.
- Middle school cross country leaves out the 1-mile races (the junior varsity race at Fort Riley's meet: "1 Mile Run 7th Grade Girls"; St. Xavier's notes for September 10: "All JV Race - 1 mile"; no other 2026 LetsGoRun meet runs one). Since November 3 this holds for every Kansas middle school (`MS_XC_EXCLUDE` in `riley-pottawatomie-ks.mjs`; Anthony and Eisenhower in `manhattan-ks-middle.mjs`): Flint Hills Christian MS's Fort Riley meet went from 13 runners to its 7.
- Junction City's home meet at Milford Lake is the "Blue Jay Invitational" on Bound and the "Bob Schmoekel Cross Country Invitational" on MileSplit Live: Kansas's alias table (`src/schools/data/ks-aliases.json`, since November 4; before, the team's `milesplit.names`) maps one to the other for every school.
- Middle and high school rule (`AGENTS.md` 5c): a past game or meet with no result anywhere is listed as played ("No score posted" / "No results posted").

## Limitations

- **Saved copies** (November 3): Junction City HS and St. Xavier keep a saved copy shared by every Cloudflare location (`globalSavedCopy`), so a build MaxPreps refuses (403 bursts) serves the last good feed even where the school is new to the cache.

- **Meets with no results anywhere** (preview, November 3): Junction City HS girls' golf October 1 (its own invitational) and September 24 (Salina Central: Prep.Golf lists Junction City without a team score); St. Xavier cross country September 10 (WCHS Invitational) and September 17 (Karr Classic): neither is on MileSplit Live or LetsGoRun; Junction City MS cross country September 12, 16 and 24. Middle school volleyball and football: no scores published (Bound has none entered). Junction City girls' tennis meets other than regional and state are not on TennisReporting; its 2026 fall regional and state draws do not list Junction City.

- **Not published** (5c): middle school game scores (Junction City MS, Fort Riley MS, St. Xavier JH: none on MaxPreps; Bound finals only where a school enters them); St. Xavier junior high's full schedule (only the entries other schools put on Bound; its own calendar is robots-disallowed); wrestling, swimming and bowling team results (no public source); Apostolic Academy (no athletics published).
- **St. Xavier's calendar days** (resolved November 4): the user enabled the Google Calendar API for the key's project and added it to the key's API restrictions (it had answered `accessNotConfigured`, then `403 forbidden` while the key was limited to the YouTube Data API). `/live/status?school=st-xavier-ks&sport=Volleyball` reads `google_calendar: read ok, events 24`. In production the calendar now fills varsity volleyball September 7 at Veritas and October 6 at Topeka (both "No score posted"); cross country Tescott (September 14) and Alma (September 19); the junior high's volleyball days (September 1 Quad at Flint Hills Christian, September 12 Chapman, October 5 Peabody, October 6 Topeka, October 13 Cornerstone at Auburn) and its cross country days (Milford State Park on September 10 with 4 runners from LetsGoRun; Tescott, Northern Heights and Alma with no results posted); and track's first spring date (March 30, 2027, at Manhattan). Calendar entries fill only the days MaxPreps and Bound leave empty. Varsity basketball is not on the calendar yet: it fills when the calendar or MaxPreps posts it.
- **St. Xavier's league:** KSHSAA's directory lists St. Xavier HS as "Independent - HS" and its junior high as "Independent - JH" (found by `scripts/onboard-hs.mjs`, November 4); the catalog's "Independent" is right.
