# Middle Tennessee school module

`src/schools/middle-tennessee.mjs` reads goblueraiders.com (SIDEARM, read directly), set up by `start-schools` on October 22, 2026 for Conference USA (ported from Charlotte's handlers; Charlotte's own rules removed). The event/result contract, caching, display and generic parsing stay shared.

Sports (10): Baseball, Basketball, Cross Country, Football, Golf, Soccer, Softball, Tennis, Track & Field, Volleyball.

## Rules

- Cross country is on the men's and women's "Track & Field/Cross Country" pages: the fall meets are cross country (TFRRS `TN_college_*_Middle_Tennessee`).
- A golf card reading "Individuals" (the Grover Page Classic, beside the JT Poston) has no team place.
- Shared Conference USA rules (`tests/conference-usa-shared.mjs`): swim duals written with the team word and no colon; golf events for individuals have no team place; the golf story pattern names the mascot; opponents "Jax State", "MTSU", "NMSU", "Western Ky.", "Sam Houston State", "Fla. International", "Kennesaw" count as members; Conference USA sponsors no field hockey, lacrosse, rowing, wrestling or gymnastics (no Conference USA record in those).

Each rule has a check in `tests/middle-tennessee-module.mjs` (generated page blocks) or `tests/conference-usa-shared.mjs`; each new shared rule was mutated and its test failed. Every football, volleyball, soccer, field hockey and hockey record on the preview equals the page's own (overall and conference, Oct 22).
